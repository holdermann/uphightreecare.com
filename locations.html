Areas We Serve in the Salt Lake Valley

From the Avenues to the Point of the Mountain, bench to west side — if you're in the Salt Lake Valley, you're in our service area.

One valley, very different trees

Tree care in the Salt Lake Valley isn’t one job. The east bench — Millcreek, Holladay, Cottonwood Heights, upper Sandy — holds giants in real canyon-wind exposure. The historic core keeps century-old canopies with city rules attached. The west side grows fast trees on practical lots. We work all of it, and the city pages above cover what matters where you live. On the edge of the valley, or just outside it? Call — we likely still cover you.
