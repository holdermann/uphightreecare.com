Tree Services in Kearns, UT

Kearns was built fast in the post-war years on the valley’s open west side, and its tree story is written in its parking strips: thousands of ash, maple, and flowering pear planted in narrow strips along the grid streets near the Utah Olympic Oval, now full-grown and lifting sidewalks, tangling in lines, and shading out lawns on the compact lots behind them.

West-side wind matters here too — storms coming over the Oquirrhs hit Kearns and Magna before anywhere else in the valley, and fence-line trees take the brunt. Our Kearns work runs to practical jobs done right: strip-tree trimming, fence-line clearance between neighbors, removals where a tree has outgrown a small lot, and stump grinding so the yard’s one shade tree can be replaced with a better one.

Working around Utah Olympic Oval, Oquirrh Park and 5400 South corridor — and every neighborhood in between.

Tree services in Kearns

Tree Removal

Stump Grinding

Kearns tree care questions

Who takes care of parking-strip trees in Kearns? +

Kearns is a metro township served by Greater Salt Lake Municipal Services, and in most of it the homeowner maintains the parking-strip tree even though the strip is public right-of-way. That means the trimming is on you — but so is the say in how it’s done. We keep strip trees healthy, off the lines, and off your sidewalk.

What does tree removal cost on a typical Kearns lot? +

Kearns removals tend to land on the affordable end for the valley: the common jobs are mid-sized strip and yard trees with straightforward front access, not canyon-bench giants. Fence-line trees shared with a neighbor add some rigging care. Every estimate is free, written, and firm — you’ll know the number before we bring a saw.
