Our tree care services

Six services, one standard: the right cut, at the right time, for the right reason. Every job starts with a free estimate and ends with a raked yard.

Tree Removal

Safe removal of dead, dying, or hazardous trees — from parking-strip maples to 80-foot cottonwoods in tight backyards.

Tree Trimming

Clearance and maintenance trimming — off the roof, away from power lines, up from the sidewalk, and looking sharp.

Tree Pruning

Surgical, health-first cuts: deadwood out, weak unions corrected, young trees trained — timed to the dormant season.

Tree Thinning

Selective canopy thinning that lets wind and light pass through — less storm load, healthier grass underneath.

Tree Shaping

Ornamental shaping, young-tree training, and patient restoration of topped or storm-damaged trees.

Stump Grinding

That stump from the tree you removed years ago? Ground below grade in under an hour, ready to re-sod or replant.
