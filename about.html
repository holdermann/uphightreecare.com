Utah’s Family-Owned Tree Care Experts

Fifteen-plus years in the Salt Lake Valley, one family's name on every job.

Up High Tree Care is a family-owned tree service based in Salt Lake City. For more than fifteen years we’ve climbed, trimmed, pruned, and removed trees across the Salt Lake Valley — from the century-old canopies of the Avenues to the wind-tested spruces of the east bench and the hard-working shade trees of the west side.

We’re not a franchise, and we’re not a crew that materialized after the last windstorm. The person who walks your property and writes your estimate is accountable for the job from first cut to final rake. That’s what family-owned means in practice: our name is on the truck, the quote, and the result.

The valley’s trees taught us the work. Canyon winds that test every limb, clay soils and hard water, fast-growing poplars planted a lifetime ago, city rules about parking-strip trees — tree care here is local knowledge as much as technique, and we’ve spent fifteen years earning it, yard by yard.

How we work

Honest assessments

We’d rather save a tree than sell a removal. If the cheaper service is the right one, that’s the one we quote.

Safety without shortcuts

Rigging, ropes, and planning on every job — and the insurance to back it. Nobody’s roof, fence, or garden pays for our speed.

Clean when we leave

Chips hauled, wood stacked or gone, lawn raked. The job isn’t done when the tree is down; it’s done when the yard looks right.

Firm written prices

The estimate is free, the number is firm, and the invoice matches it. That’s how we’d want to be treated.

Licensed, insured, and local

Full liability and workers’ compensation coverage on every job — ask for proof of insurance and we’ll send it before you book. Reach us at (801) 890-7432 or info@uphightreecare.com.

Put a name you can call to your trees

Tell us about the tree. We’ll walk the property, give you a firm written price, and you decide — no pressure, no obligation.
